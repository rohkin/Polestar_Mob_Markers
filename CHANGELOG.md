# Changelog

## 0.0.2

- **Only Polestar's current step** (new option, on by default): markers show just the quests the guide step you're on works on, instead of your whole quest log. With no guide running, the whole log shows as before. The markers follow the guide as it moves on.
- **Finished objectives drop off**: a mob stops being marked as soon as its objective is done, not only when the whole quest is.
- **Tidier world map**: nearby spawns of the same mob merge into one marker more readily when zoomed out. Zoom in to see them all; the legend still counts every spawn.
- Note: mobs you'd hidden from the legend may point at a different mob once after updating. Click the row again to fix it.

## 0.0.1

- First release: markers for every spawn of the mobs your active quests need, on the world map and minimap, with a legend, colour palettes, sizes and opacity options, and a minimap button.
